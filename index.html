02  ·  Problems

Four problems found on the live Isharya PDP

Found by auditing the live Tennis Girlie Square Front Back Earrings page on desktop and mobile, its structured data and its Google PageSpeed report. Each maps to specific modules in the redesign that follows.

PROBLEM 01  ·  PRODUCT JUDGEMENT
Solved by modules 01, 06, 07 →

A shopper can't judge size, sparkle or fit from the page

Earrings are bought on how they look on the ear. The live page shows five still images, no video and no way to judge scale. The facts that answer "how big is it?" exist, but they're hidden.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  BURIED INFORMATION

Size and weight are locked inside a collapsed accordion

"Weight: 4.2gms, Dimension: 30 X 24 mm" only appears after opening Description. The same block reads "0.3 Miron Gold Plating", a typo for micron, on the one line that describes the plating quality.

MEDIA

No video, no on-ear scale, no styling context

There is nothing that shows the front-back construction moving, how the piece sits on the lobe, or what to wear it with.

Live PDP, desktop. Three collapsed accordions hold the description, size, care and shipping.
PROBLEM 02  ·  TRUST AND PROOF
Solved by modules 04, 05, 10 →

There is no proof on the page that other people bought and loved this

Fashion jewellery at ₹3,599 is a considered purchase, and the first questions are "is it good quality?" and "will it irritate my ears?". The live page answers both with four small badges and nothing else.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  SOCIAL PROOF

Zero reviews, zero ratings, no celebrity or customer imagery

No review widget, no star rating, and no customer or celebrity photos anywhere on the page.

SEARCH VISIBILITY

Google can't show price or stars for this product

The page's structured data declares a WebPage, a BreadcrumbList and a JewelryStore, but no Product with price, availability or rating. Search results and AI shopping agents get no product facts to show.

PROBLEM 03  ·  BUYING FRICTION
Solved by modules 02, 03, 04, 12 →

Price, payment and delivery questions go unanswered at the moment of decision

PRICING

Three price signals and an offer in hard-to-read type

MRP ₹3,999 struck through, ₹3,599, "Save 10%", then a separate green bar reading "Extra 150/- Off On Prepaid Orders" in tiny, widely letter-spaced type squeezed between the two buttons.

DELIVERY AND PAYMENT

No delivery date, no PIN check, no EMI

Shipping sits in a collapsed accordion that only says free shipping above ₹1,499. There is no "when will it arrive" and no EMI or COD information before checkout.

MOBILE

The buy buttons scroll away on mobile

Once a shopper scrolls past the buttons there is no sticky add to cart. The only floating element is the WhatsApp button.

GIFTING

03  ·  The Redesign

Twelve modules, each with a job

Every module below solves one of the problems above. For each one: why it's on the page (the conversion reason) and why it looks the way it does (the design reason). The visual language follows Isharya's own site: Epilogue and Work Sans, the burgundy accent, square edges and hairline rules, so the page feels like Isharya, only clearer.

Desktop, first screen. Gallery on the left stays pinned while the details scroll.
01

Sticky, video-first gallery

Solves → Problem 01, product judgement
WHY IT'S THERE
  • Video leads. The first slide is a short product video, so the front-back construction and sparkle are visible before any scrolling.
  • On-model shots in the thumbnail strip. Two of six thumbnails show the piece on the ear, the fastest way to read scale.
  • Zoom, save and share sit on the image itself, where the intent happens.
DESIGN REASON
  • The image stays pinned while the right column scrolls, so the product is always in view next to the detail being read. It releases when the buy section ends.
  • A 68px vertical thumbnail strip keeps the main image as large as possible on desktop. On mobile it becomes a horizontal strip under the image.
  • A "Bestseller" badge in the brand burgundy is the only colour on the image, so it reads without competing with the gold.
Buy box. One price, stock and EMI, one primary action.
02

A buy box with one price and one primary action

Solves → Problem 03, buying friction
WHY IT'S THERE
  • One price, stated once. The live page shows a struck MRP, a sale price, a percentage and a separate prepaid offer. The redesign shows a single number marked "Inclusive of all taxes", so there's no mental maths.
  • Stock and EMI under the price. Low-stock urgency and "EMI from Rs.333 per month" answer "should I buy now?" and "can I afford it?" in the same glance.
  • "Visit store and try on in-person" gives hesitant shoppers an offline path instead of leaving.
DESIGN REASON
  • Clear button hierarchy. Buy Now is the only solid black button, Add to Cart is outlined, and the store link is smaller still.
  • The price is the largest text on the page after the title, set in Epilogue, Isharya's display face.
Pay via row. Five methods, evenly spaced, aligned to the row labels.
03

Payment methods, answered before checkout

Solves → Problem 03, buying friction
WHY IT'S THERE

"Can I pay the way I want?" is one of the last questions before a shopper commits. UPI, credit card, net banking, COD and EMI are shown right under the buttons, instead of being discovered at checkout.

DESIGN REASON
  • Its own labelled row, "PAY VIA", in the same left rail as PRICE and CHECK, so the whole buy box scans like a spec sheet.
  • Thin line icons in muted grey, equal columns, so the row is informative but never louder than the Buy Now button above it.
Check and Cert rows. Delivery and skin-safety, side by side with the price.
04

Delivery check and certified materials

Solves → Problem 02, trust and Problem 03, friction
WHY IT'S THERE
  • PIN code check. Arrival date is the top question for anything bought for an occasion. Free delivery, 7-day returns and secure payments sit right under the field.
  • Certified materials, in plain words. Hypoallergenic, nickel free, anti-tarnish and a 1-year warranty directly address "will this irritate my ears?" and "will it tarnish?".
DESIGN REASON
  • Burgundy line icons with sentence-case labels, matching the style of Isharya's current trust badges, but drawn as vectors so they stay sharp and legible at any size.
  • A 2×2 grid with generous spacing instead of four small boxed badges, so each claim reads in one glance.
Seen on and Notes rows. Social proof, then three reasons to buy.
05

Celebrity styling and curated notes

Solves → Problem 02, trust and proof
WHY IT'S THERE
  • "Seen on" puts celebrity styling right beside the buy box. Aspiration is one of the strongest trust signals in fashion jewellery.
  • "Why it stands out" gives three short reasons (silhouette, weight, packaging) for shoppers who skim rather than read a paragraph.
DESIGN REASON

Three equal image tiles with the edit name set on the photo keep the row compact. The notes use numbered hairline rows, the same rhythm as the rest of the panel, so the column stays calm.

Spec and Gift rows. The hidden size facts, now the most visible numbers in the panel.
06

Dimensions up front, and a gift toggle

Solves → Problem 01, judgement and Problem 04, order value
WHY IT'S THERE
  • 30mm × 24mm, 4.2g, sold as a pair. The exact facts buried in the live accordion, now visible without a click, with a care guide link beside them.
  • "Send as a gift" adds a recipient, a message and premium wrapping, and ships with no price on the invoice.
DESIGN REASON

Four equal tiles with a large number and a small label read like a spec card, so size is understood at a glance. The gift option is a simple toggle rather than a form, so it costs nothing to ignore.

07

"See how it comes alive": the on-ear story

Solves → Problem 01, product judgement
Experience the product. Three chapters with an inline add to cart underneath.
WHY IT'S THERE

Close-up, on-ear photography in three chapters (The Detail, The Proportion, The Ritual) shows the marquise stones, how the front-back piece frames the lobe, and how it's worn. It's the closest thing to holding the earring up to a mirror.

An inline "Ready to make it yours?" bar with the price and returns promise catches the shopper at the peak of interest.

DESIGN REASON

A full-width, cinematic stage breaks the rhythm after the dense buy box and signals a change from facts to feeling. Frosted glass chapter tabs sit on the photo, so switching chapters never pulls attention away from the product.

08

Four ways to style it

Solves → Problem 01, judgement and Problem 04, order value
Styling inspiration. Tennis Whites, Sharp Tailoring, After Dark, The Stack.
WHY IT'S THERE

"What would I wear it with?" is a hidden objection. Four looks, from sporty to evening to stacked, show the earring works across occasions, which widens the reason to buy and sets up the complete-the-look bundle right below.

DESIGN REASON

A centred, four-tile gallery with the same model across looks keeps attention on the earring changing context, not on a new face. Each tile is labelled as an AI styling concept, to stay transparent with the shopper.

09

Complete the look, with bundle pricing

Solves → Problem 04, order value
Complete the look. Four Tennis Girlie pieces with two bundle tiers.
WHY IT'S THERE

The live page shows ten unrelated bestsellers. The redesign shows four pieces from the same Tennis Girlie line (hoops, pendant, stack ring, bracelet) with "Buy any 2" and "Buy any 3" pricing applied automatically at checkout. It gives a concrete reason to add a second piece and ties into Isharya's existing spend-based offers.

DESIGN REASON

Two offer cards sit above the products, with the three-piece tier marked "Most chosen" to anchor the higher option. One-tap "Add" buttons keep the shopper on the PDP instead of sending her to four more pages.

Questions, answered. Five objections, handled on the page.
10

FAQ built from real objections

Solves → Problem 02, trust and Problem 03, friction
WHY IT'S THERE

Care of the gold plating, what happens if they don't suit me, sensitive ears, COD availability and gifting: the five questions a shopper would otherwise ask on WhatsApp or leave to find answers for.

DESIGN REASON

Collapsed, question-first rows keep the section short. Answers are written in plain text in the page, so search engines and AI assistants can read them too.

Service strip. Five promises, one line each.
11

A closing service promise

Solves → Problem 02, trust
WHY IT'S THERE

7-day returns, 1-year warranty, free shipping, gift packaging and hypoallergenic materials, restated at the bottom for the shopper who scrolled the whole page and needs one last reassurance.

DESIGN REASON

A big number or word ("7", "1", "Free", "Box", "Safe") over a short label makes each promise scannable from a distance.

Mobile. Buy box, and the sticky bar that follows the scroll.
12

Mobile-first buy flow with a sticky bar

Solves → Problem 03, buying friction
WHY IT'S THERE

Most paid traffic lands on mobile. Once the buy buttons scroll away, a sticky bar with the product name, price, Add to Cart and Buy Now stays at the bottom of the screen, so the decision is always one tap away.

DESIGN REASON
  • Frosted glass bar that lets the page show through, so it feels part of the page rather than a banner.
  • It only appears once the main buttons leave the screen, so there are never two sets of buttons visible at once.
  • Title pinned over the image on mobile, so the shopper knows what she's looking at from the first screen.
BEFORE LAUNCH  ·  PLACEHOLDERS TO REPLACE WITH REAL DATA
  • Ratings and reviews. "4.8 / 5 · 124 reviews" is a design placeholder. The live page has no reviews yet, so this needs a reviews app connected to real customer data.
  • Stock and purchase notifications. "Only 7 left · Selling fast in Delhi NCR" and "bought this 22 minutes ago" must come from live inventory and order data, or be removed.
  • 360° view. The four rotation frames are currently the same image and need a real turntable shoot.
  • Price. The design shows Rs.3,999. The live sale price is ₹3,599, so the final price display should follow Isharya's current pricing.
